Why Is FDE Needed?

THE PROBLEM OFTEN LIVES BETWEEN TEAMS.

Each team can be technically correct — while the customer’s end-to-end workflow is still broken.

Ownership GapSomeone must follow the customer outcome across team boundaries.
Integration RealityLegacy systems, APIs, messy data, permissions and unique workflows create last-mile problems.
Production RealityA demo can pass while production fails because of configuration, scale or real data.
AI Needs JudgmentAI can accelerate code, but correctness, risk and business impact still need ownership.
Adoption MattersDeployment is not success if users do not adopt the solution or KPIs do not improve.
Learning Must CompoundRepeated field problems should become reusable tools, patterns and product improvements.
